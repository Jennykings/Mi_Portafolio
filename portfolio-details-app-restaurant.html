Restaurant Reservations App

Developed with Android Studio, this app allows users to reserve tables at their favorite restaurant from their Android devices. Using Kotlin and Java, the app offers an intuitive interface to select the date, time, and number of people, as well as to manage reservations and receive confirmations in real time. The app includes features such as restaurant search, availability display, and pre-booking management. With an attractive and functional design, it provides a smooth and efficient experience for users looking to simplify their dining plans.
